# LMG English EF: Survey Time

A one-page classroom survey: students estimate six real statistics about how parents' divorce relates to teenagers' lives (slider 1–100), then see how close they were.

- Static site (`index.html`, no dependencies), served by GitHub Pages.
- No names, no accounts, no server. Only the latest result is stored in the browser's `localStorage`.

## Sources of the six figures

| # | Figure | Source |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | 31 of 100 Dutch young people had experienced parental divorce by about age 19 (21 by about age 11) | Tullius, De Kroon, Almansa & Reijneveld (2022), *Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry*, TRAILS, N = 2,230. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-020-01715-0 |
| 2 | Psychological help, US teens 12–16: 21% (divorced) vs 11% (intact) → +10 | Zill, Morrison & Coiro (1993), as summarised by Dept. of Justice Canada (2002). https://justice.gc.ca/eng/rp-pr/fl-lf/divorce/2002_2/p2.html |
| 3 | Poor relationship with father, ages 12–16: 32% vs 14% → +18 | Zill et al. (1993), N = 1,147; same review, https://justice.gc.ca/eng/rp-pr/fl-lf/divorce/2002_2/p3.html |
| 4 | Poor relationship with mother, ages 18–22: 25% vs 18% → +7 (at 12–16: 8% vs 8%) | Zill et al. (1993); same review |
| 5 | Serious social/emotional/psychological problems: ~25% vs 10% → +15 (75–80% of children of divorce do fine) | Hetherington & Kelly (2002), *For Better or for Worse*, as reported by The Cavalier Daily, 6 Feb 2002 (press coverage, not the book itself). https://www.cavalierdaily.com/article/2002/02/professor-publishes-book-on-divorce |
| 6 | High-school completion: about 4% lower probability after parental divorce (college: 7%) | Brand, Moore, Song & Xie (2019), *PNAS* 116(15), NLSY79, N = 5,176. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6462058/ |

## Caveats

- Most figures are US data from the 1980s–2000s; they are group averages and do not prove cause and effect.
- Figure 5 comes from press coverage of the book and one outlet quoted a slightly different number (about 22%) in another interview.
- Figure 6 is reported in the paper as a "4% lower probability"; it is treated here as roughly 4 per 100.
